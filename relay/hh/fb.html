Why this guide, why now

The beach is one strand. The question is which access.

Every Hilton Head beach is the same twelve miles of sand. What changes is what's behind it: restrooms close to the towel, lifeguards in season, a playground or a shady pavilion, and whether parking costs $0 or $5 an hour. The guide ranks the seven accesses that earn a family's day.

Two things changed in 2026

Islanders Beach Park is closed until June 2027 for a rebuild, and every Town beach park except Coligny now meters parking at $3 an hour on weekdays and $5 on weekends. Most "best beaches" lists online still say otherwise. This guide doesn't.

The parts nobody tells you

Beach rules families actually ask about

The Town's rules are written for lawyers. The guide translates the ones that matter when you have kids, a dog and a wagon full of gear.

Dogs
No dogs 10 to 5 in summer

Friday before Memorial Day through Labor Day, dogs are off the beach from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. The guide has the spring, fall and winter rules too.

Holes & shovels
Fill every hole before you leave

Holes deeper than 12 inches are illegal; shovels must be plastic or wood and under 30 inches. Sea turtles and lifeguard trucks are the reason.

Not allowed
No glass, no alcohol, no fires

Plus no boogie boards or balls inside the lifeguard swim zones April to September. Fines run past $1,000, and they do write them.
