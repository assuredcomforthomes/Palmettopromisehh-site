Graduation week, in order

Wednesday, Thursday, Friday — and what each one is for

Every time below comes from the Depot's own visitor pages. The guide tells you what to do with them.

WED

Orientation

Check in by 9:30 · Tour 10–12:30 · Brief 1:30

The Marine Corps 101 tour and the family brief at the Douglas Visitors' Center. Then Bay Street for dinner, and the picnic shopping on the way home.

THU

Family Day

Run 7:00 · Ceremony 9:30 · Liberty 10–3

Your first look at your recruit on the Motivational Run, then five hours of liberty. They can't leave the Depot — the guide explains how to make those hours count.

FRI

Graduation

Colors 7:45 · Ceremony 9:00 · Done by 10

Morning Colors, the ceremony on Peatross Parade Deck, and the moment you walk off base with a Marine. Why your flight shouldn't be before 4.
