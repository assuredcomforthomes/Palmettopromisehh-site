Why golf on Hilton Head

One island. Twenty-plus courses.

Hilton Head packs more than twenty courses onto twelve miles of island, designed by Dye, Nicklaus, Jones, Fazio and Hills — and most of them are open to visitors.

01

Home of the RBC Heritage

Harbour Town Golf Links hosts the PGA Tour every April — and the rest of the year, you can play the same 18 holes the pros do, lighthouse finish included.

02

Ocean on one side, marsh on the other

Fairways run along the Atlantic dunes and through tidal marsh and live oaks — the scenery is the reason people come back every year.

Play it year-round

Mild winters and shoulder-season rates make spring and fall the locals' favorite time to tee it up.
